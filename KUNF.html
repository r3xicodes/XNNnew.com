Kolovoas Union Naval Force Formally Established at Porta Fredronika

Xana Colony of Kolovoas officially establishes its own naval service, marking a significant step in the development of the Empire's newest eastern territory

Photo of the KUNF at sea

TKO
Imperial Affairs Desk
Xara Palace, Seravell

PORTA FREDRONIKA, XANA COLONY OF KOLOVOAS — The Xana Colony of Kolovoas has officially established its own naval service with the creation of the Kolovoas Union Naval Force (KUNF), marking a significant step in the development of the Empire's newest eastern territory.

The announcement was made during a formal ceremony at Porta Fredronika, where colonial officials, sailors, representatives of the Xana National Navy, and members of the colonial administration gathered for the commissioning of the new force.

The establishment of the KUNF comes as Kolovoas increasingly develops into a major eastern maritime hub for the Great Holy Xanarcica Empire. With merchant vessels, exploration ships, and military traffic now regularly traveling between Xara and Kolovoas, colonial authorities determined that a permanent local naval force was necessary.

"Our Waters, Our Responsibility"

Speaking during the ceremony, Flora Empress Arc X. Xana, the reigning Empress of the Great Holy Xanarcica Empire, addressed the assembled personnel through a recorded imperial statement from Xara Palace.

"Kolovoas was born upon the sea. Its future will be secured upon the sea. Today, we do not merely commission ships. We entrust the people of Kolovoas with the responsibility of protecting their waters."

The Empress continued:

"The Union Naval Force shall serve beside the Xana National Navy, not beneath its shadow. Let its sailors carry the name of Kolovoas with discipline, courage, and honor."

Her statement was followed by the raising of the KUNF ensign over the newly designated naval facilities at Porta Fredronika.

A Local Naval Force

The Kolovoas Union Naval Force will initially focus on coastal defense, maritime patrol, search and rescue, anti-smuggling operations, and the protection of commercial shipping operating around the colony.

The force will maintain close operational ties with the Xana National Navy, which will provide training, logistical assistance, and naval doctrine during the KUNF's initial development.

The first Commander of the KUNF, Commodore Elias Taren, spoke to XNN following the ceremony.

"We are a young navy. There is no shame in that. Every navy begins somewhere, and ours begins here, at Porta Fredronika."

— Commodore Elias Taren

When asked what he considered the force's most important responsibility, Taren answered simply:

"Making sure every ship that leaves this harbor has a safe route home."

The First KUNF Fleet

The inaugural force currently consists of a small collection of patrol and support vessels intended to establish a permanent maritime presence around Kolovoas.

Initial capabilities include:

  • Coastal patrol vessels
  • Harbor patrol craft
  • Search-and-rescue vessels
  • Maritime survey ships
  • Communications and support vessels

Additional ships are expected to enter service as the colony's population, trade network, and infrastructure continue to expand.

XNN correspondent Leria Vann reported from the waterfront as the first vessels were formally commissioned.

"The harbor is still surrounded by construction. New warehouses are being raised, roads are being extended, and ships arrive almost every day. Yet today, for the first time, there is a naval flag flying over Porta Fredronika that belongs specifically to Kolovoas."

A New Maritime Identity

The establishment of the KUNF also represents the development of a distinct colonial identity within the wider Xana imperial system.

While Kolovoas remains firmly under the sovereignty of the Xana Crown, officials described the Union Naval Force as a means of giving the colony greater responsibility over its own maritime affairs.

A Ministry of Colonial Affairs representative told XNN:

"Kolovoas is no longer simply a settlement at the edge of the map. It is becoming a permanent part of the Empire's eastern presence."

As the commissioning ceremony concluded, KUNF sailors marched along the waterfront while visiting XNN vessels sounded their horns from the harbor.

Above them, the new naval ensign remained raised over Porta Fredronika.

For the sailors of Kolovoas, their first day had begun.

Their navy had finally arrived.

XNN — Reporting from Porta Fredronika, Xana Colony of Kolovoas.
