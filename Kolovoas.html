Xana Colony of Kolovoas Established in the Eastern Ocean

The Great Holy Xanarcica Empire announces the founding of a new overseas territory discovered during an imperial exploration initiative

Strategic Operation Plan of Op. Twin Tides

TKO
Imperial Affairs Desk
Xara Palace, Seravell

SERAVELL, XARA — The Great Holy Xanarcica Empire has officially announced the establishment of the Xana Colony of Kolovoas, a newly founded overseas territory in the Eastern Ocean discovered during a recent imperial exploration initiative.

The announcement was made from Xara Palace in Seravell by Flora Empress Arc X. Xana, who described the colony as the beginning of a new era of exploration, commerce, and maritime expansion for the Empire.

Unlike Poliskioa and Coronias, which entered the Empire through military campaigns and stabilization operations, Kolovoas was founded through exploration and settlement. The colony represents the Empire's first major eastern foothold beyond traditional maritime routes.

"The spirit of Xana has never been confined by horizons. Kolovoas stands as proof that the future belongs not only to those who defend their lands, but also to those willing to discover new ones."

— Flora Empress Arc X. Xana

According to the Ministry of Exploration and Colonial Affairs, the island was identified during a long-range survey expedition dispatched to chart eastern waters and identify potential trade corridors.

Imperial surveyors reported favorable conditions for permanent settlement, including natural harbors, freshwater sources, fertile land, and ample room for future expansion.

A Gateway to the East

Military and economic planners have already begun describing Kolovoas as a strategic gateway for future eastern trade.

The colony's location provides a vital resupply point for vessels operating beyond the Empire's traditional sphere of navigation and offers a foundation for future diplomatic and commercial ventures.

Admiral Cassian Valerius of the Xana National Navy spoke to XNN shortly after returning from the expedition.

"Kolovoas gives us reach. Every chart we draw from here, every route we establish, pushes the frontier of imperial knowledge farther east."

— Admiral Cassian Valerius

Construction crews arriving with the first wave of settlers have already begun work on a colonial harbor, administrative district, warehouses, and defensive infrastructure.

A member of the engineering corps described the pace of development:

"When we arrived there was only coastline and wilderness. Now ships are docking every week. The colony is growing faster than anyone expected."

— Imperial Engineering Corps

First Colonial Administration

The Crown has established a provisional colonial administration to oversee development and settlement operations until a permanent governing structure is finalized.

Officials indicate that population growth is expected to accelerate as merchants, engineers, farmers, and maritime workers relocate from Xara and other imperial territories.

Minister of Colonial Affairs Armand Vexis commented:

"Kolovoas is not merely a settlement. It is an investment in the future of the Empire."

— Minister Armand Vexis

The Beginning of a New Era

The establishment of Kolovoas marks the opening of what officials are calling the Eastern Exploration Initiative, a long-term effort aimed at expanding trade networks, maritime knowledge, and imperial presence across previously uncharted waters.

Speaking from a balcony overlooking the capital, Flora Empress Arc X. Xana concluded the ceremony with a message directed toward the Empire's sailors and settlers:

"Beyond every horizon lies another. Kolovoas is not the end of our journey eastward. It is the beginning."

— Flora Empress Arc X. Xana

As settlers continue arriving and construction progresses across the island, the Xana Colony of Kolovoas stands as the Empire's newest territory and the first step into a vast eastern ocean waiting to be explored.

XNN will continue to provide updates from Kolovoas as development of the colony advances.
